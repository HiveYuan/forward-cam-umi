State of the tile-free head-cam UMI work when Yuan left: which checkpoints to run, where the data

## Summary

- **SLAM labels match tile labels.** In the same relative action space, with the same state, the SLAM-labelled
  policy (exp053, D2a) and the tile-labelled one (exp059) are level on the 6 robot-val episodes: val loss 0.447 vs
  0.446, chunk-mean position error 71.1 vs 73.1 mm. One seed each.
- **The remaining gap to the paper's G48 (65.2 mm) comes from the state.** With the tool pose in the robot base as
  state (D1, exp051, tile labels) the error is 63.2 mm. The D2a state (pose since the episode start) is what a
  tile-free pipeline can give for the glove half.
- **The new yuan kitchen sessions are usable.** With the parallax-gated scale (`Bpar`) their labels are as accurate
  as the paper sessions' (1 s median 3.5 vs 4.4 mm against the tile labels). 134 of 270 recorded episodes pass all
  gates. Training D2a with them (exp060, teleop sampled at 21%) keeps the lab accuracy (70.3 mm) and cuts the error on
  held-out kitchen episodes from 108.6 to 46.4 mm.
- **Nothing has run on the robot yet.** All numbers are offline (robot-val episodes of the teleop set).

## Checkpoints

Lab bucket: `gs://hawaii-robotica-data/yuan/slam-cam-umi/checkpoints/`. Each folder holds
`checkpoints/<step>/pretrained_model/` (LeRobot 0.5.2, Yosub's fork lerobot_alohamini 0cae109f). The `README.md`
there maps folders to experiments.

| folder | experiment | glove labels | training data | best step | robot val | chunk mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `rel_Gslam48_startstate_20261114` | [exp053](../experiments/exp053_rel_Gslam48_startstate) (D2a) | SLAM | 48 teleop + 194 HHs3 glove | 48000 | 0.447 | 71.1 mm |
| `rel_Gtile48_startstate_20261117` | [exp059](../experiments/exp059_rel_Gtile48_startstate) | tiles (FK of HHs3 joints) | same as exp053 | 48000 | 0.446 | 73.1 mm |
| `rel_Gslam48yuan_teleop21_startstate_20261117` | [exp060](../experiments/exp060_rel_Gslam48yuan_teleop21_startstate) | SLAM | exp053's + 121 yuan kitchen episodes, teleop sampled at 21% | 50000 | 0.443 | 70.3 mm |

Reference: the paper's joint-space G48 (our rerun exp049, val 0.291) has 65.2 mm on the same episodes.

All three share actions and state, so one deployment path runs them:
- **Action:** tool pose at t+j relative to the current tool pose (translation + 6D rotation) plus the absolute
  gripper; chunk 100.
- **State:** tool pose relative to the pose at the episode (rollout) start, plus the gripper. At deployment both
  poses are FK of the measured joints, so no tiles are needed.
- **Deployment:** [`slam/deploy_rel.py`](../slam/deploy_rel.py). It turns each chunk into base-frame targets
  (FK(now) composed with the prediction), ensembles chunks in the base frame (LeRobot's own temporal ensembler must
  stay off) and runs grasp_site IK. Offline replay commands are in its docstring; the deployment notes are in
  [`experiments/glove_gain/README.md`](../experiments/glove_gain/README.md#deployment-slamdeploy_rel-eval_grid).
  Only exp053 has been replayed offline (IK converges on all ticks, 1 ms each). The ensembling coefficient still has
  to be chosen on the robot.

Suggested robot order: exp053 and exp059 side by side (does the tile-vs-SLAM tie hold in success rate?), then
exp060.

## Pipeline defaults in one line each

- Masks `sam3_v2_head`; SLAM `droid_adaptive` (DROID-SLAM, per-episode segments with a 5 s preroll, 1 s where the
  head turns faster than 120 deg/s before the episode).
- Scale: `Bnet` on DA3METRIC-LARGE with the camera K (no ball). For the yuan sessions `Bpar`: the same, from keyframes
  with baseline/depth >= 0.1 only. The yuan heads translated about half as much as in the paper sessions, and DROID's
  keyframe depth is too far at small parallax. `Bpar` is slightly worse on the paper sessions (5.7 vs 4.9%), so the
  global default is still `Bnet`.
- Gripper from the head camera (`slam.head_width`); the wrist camera is a cross-check only.

## Open items

1. **Robot evaluation** of exp053 / exp059 / exp060. A robot for Yuan is being discussed with Igor.
2. **State gap.** D1's base-frame state (63.2 mm) needs tiles for the glove half. A tile-free state that carries
   the same information is the main open modelling question.
3. **Yuan data tail.** p90 scale error is still about 60% on a minority of segments (SLAM or tile-fit failures;
   tile-fit residual 15 to 51 mm). A tile-free per-episode filter has not been tried.
4. **Recording habits** seen in the yuan data, for the next collection: approach with the jaws open as the robot
   does (yuan demos open 33% of the time vs 70% in HHs3); keep the gripper in the head camera's view; keep the
   block inside the tiles; a slow sideways head movement of a few cm helps the scale, fast turns (> 120 deg/s) hurt.
5. **Seeds.** Every arm has one seed. Differences of a few mm are within checkpoint-to-checkpoint noise.
6. **RSS abstract** due 2026-12-04, while Yuan is away.
